FAQ

Before you sign up.

When do the courses start?
No dates yet. We are building the material now and the waiting list is how we decide the order to finish them in — the track with the most interest gets made first.
What will they cost?
Not set. The waiting list is not a commitment to buy, and nobody on it is charged anything. You will see the price before you are asked to decide.
Are these live or self-paced?
Self-paced video and written lessons, with a monthly live Q&A so you can bring your own project and get it unstuck.
Will the material go out of date?
Parts of it, quickly — that is the nature of these tools. Enrolment includes twelve months of updates, and we re-record the lessons that break rather than leaving them up.
Do I need to take them in order?
No. They are four separate tracks, not four levels. Visualisation and Real Estate assume no technical background; Real-Time 3D and Urban Digital Twins assume you already model.
What is the difference between Real-Time 3D and Urban Digital Twins?
Real-Time 3D is about the model and the engine: getting your project into Unreal, lit, interactive and packaged. Urban Digital Twins is about the place: coordinates, open city data, attribute joins, live feeds and analysis. If you want a walkthrough of a building, take the first. If you need to answer questions about a district, take the second.
Can you run this for our team instead?
Yes — we deliver these as private workshops, tailored to the software and projects your team actually works on. Mention it in the waiting-list note and we will come back to you.
What is the catch with the waiting list?
There isn't one. We email you when a course has a date and a price. No drip campaign, and you can leave with one click.
