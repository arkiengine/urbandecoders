Mode 1

CSS panel — no still needed

What NCA actually does: the monitor is drawn in CSS, so it stays crisp at any size and the clip lands in the glass exactly. Three clips cross-fade into each other.

<div class="screen screen--css"
     data-clips="a.mp4|b.mp4|c.mp4">
  <div class="screen__panel">
    <div class="screen__frame">
      <div class="screen__port"></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="screen__stand"></div>
</div>

Mode 2

Pinned to a photo

A still of a real screen, with the clip corner-pinned onto the glass. The four corners are percentages of the image; the transform is solved from them, so any angle lines up.

<div class="screen screen--pin"
     data-clips="a.mp4"
     data-corners="25.00,7.52 88.36,0.63 88.36,69.92 25.00,68.42"
     data-aspect="1.7778">
  <img class="screen__still" src="still.jpg" alt="">
  <div class="screen__port"></div>
</div>

Corners come from tools/screen-calibrate.html — drag four handles, copy the attribute.
