Services

Digital twins

A living model of the site, district or city.

Terrain, parcels, networks, constraints and buildings assembled from GIS, BIM and open data into one georeferenced base in a stated CRS, then served as a web explorer anyone can open from a link. As planned, or as the place runs today.

  • InRVT, IFC, 3DM, SKP, DWG, GPKG, GeoJSON, point clouds, 3D Tiles
  • BaseContext layers, site or scheme massing, web explorer, full handover
  • ScaleSite under 5 ha, district to 100 ha, city above, same base throughout

Planning & masterplanning

Test the scheme before you defend it.

Massing, density, phasing, solar, views and transit access modelled on the real site and compared option by option, with the numbers recomputing as the parameters move. Verified views and visual impact material for the application, so the case you take to committee is evidence, not artwork.

  • ScenariosDensity, height, phasing and mix side by side in one view
  • AnalysisSolar, viewshed, verified views, transit access, shadows at any hour
  • EvidenceGFA, coverage and mix recomputed live, exported to the planning file

Live city twins

The city as it runs, not just as it's drawn.

We connect the feeds a place already produces, traffic counters and signal systems, bus and rail real-time data, CCTV analytics, air-quality monitors, footfall sensors and building systems, and draw them onto the real place, updating as it happens, with the history behind it so patterns become decisions.

  • ConnectTfGM and Bee Network, Network Rail Darwin, DEFRA AURN, SCOOT and UTMC, council CCTV, LoRaWAN, your own databases
  • GetLive layers on the twin, a place dashboard, alerts on thresholds, a time scrubber to replay any day
  • HandledCCTV as anonymised counts only, every feed under the data owner's terms, built for public-sector procurement and audit

Engagement & decision

Consultation people actually take part in.

Residents explore the proposal from their own street, compare it with what is there today, and pin comments to the place they mean. Where it runs

One model. Four ways to put it in the room.

City explorer

A hosted web explorer for your place: layers, storylines and points of interest from one link, any device, no install.

Media table and kiosks

A large touch surface for consultation events, exhibitions and committee rooms, plus permanent visitor-centre installations.

Headset and walkthroughs

First-person and drone walkthroughs from the twin, as a web build or a VR piece for launches and public events.

Place dashboard

Once the place opens, the same twin runs it: assets, occupancy, environment and footfall in one view with alerts on thresholds.

Software integration

Works with the tools you already use.

A twin is only useful if it takes what you already have. We read Revit, Rhino and Grasshopper models, SketchUp, AutoCAD and 3ds Max files, QGIS layers, Blender scenes and Excel schedules into one georeferenced base, and hand results back in the same formats.

  • In RVT, IFC, 3DM, GH, SKP, DWG, DXF, MAX, FBX, GPKG, GeoJSON, SHP, XLSX
  • Out GLB, FBX, 3D Tiles, IFC, GeoJSON, XLSX schedules, PNG and MP4 from the twin
  • Live Grasshopper parameters and Excel mixes driving the model as sliders
