Digital Twins

One shared source of truth for the design team, the client and the authority — and, for the city that already exists, a base the live feeds plug straight into.

Live City Twins

The city as it runs, not just as it's drawn.

Most twins stop at the plan. Ours can carry the live city on top: we connect the APIs and feeds a place already produces — traffic counters and signal systems, bus and rail real-time data, CCTV analytics, air-quality monitors, footfall sensors, energy and building-management systems — and visualise them in the real 3D place, updating as it happens.

For a council or operator that means one screen where the whole town is legible: where the traffic is, which routes are late, where people are gathering, what the air is doing — and the history behind it, so patterns become decisions.

What we connect

Open and operator APIs — TfGM / Bee Network, Network Rail Darwin, DEFRA AURN, council traffic (SCOOT/UTMC) and CCTV systems, LoRaWAN and building-management sensors, and your own databases.

What you get

A georeferenced 3D twin with live layers, a place-management dashboard on top, alerts on thresholds, and a time-scrubber to replay any day. Web, media table or ops-room wall.

Handled properly

CCTV is processed to anonymised counts and flows — no faces stored. Every feed sits under the data owner's terms, and the platform is built for public-sector procurement, security and audit.

Products

Things we build on top of the twin.

Once a place is twinned, the same model drives everything people actually touch — in the room, on the web, and long after the launch.

City Explorer

A hosted web explorer for your place — layers, storylines and points of interest anyone can navigate from a link, on any device, with no install and no training.

Media Table

A large-format interactive table for the consultation event, exhibition or committee room — the plan as something a group can stand around, drive together and argue over.

Place Management Platform

A live dashboard on the twin — traffic, transport, CCTV counts, air quality, occupancy and footfall in one view, so whoever runs the place can see how it actually performs.

VR Experiences

Guided headset journeys through the scheme for launches, exhibitions and public events — the closest anyone gets to walking the place before it exists.

Gamified Learning

Game-engine tools that teach a place — school programmes, public exhibitions and training that make planning and the built environment something people want to explore.

Installations & Kiosks

Visitor-centre installations, wayfinding kiosks and projected pieces — permanent fixtures that keep telling the story of a place long after opening day.
