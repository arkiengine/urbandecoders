What we do

Interactive experiences.

Four ways to put a scheme in someone's hands, all drawn from one georeferenced model: a real-time twin in Unreal Engine 5, a website that carries the model rather than screenshots of it, a 360° tour you can stand inside, and a map of every layer on the real ground.

(01)

Digital twins on Unreal Engine 5

Walk it, don't watch it.

A georeferenced model of the site, district or city running in Unreal Engine 5: terrain, context, networks and the proposal in one scene, at real coordinates in a stated CRS. Because it is real-time, a client, a committee or a resident moves through it themselves instead of watching a flythrough. Hand it over as a hosted link, a desktop build, or pixel-streamed so it opens in any browser with no install.

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Real-time, not pre-rendered
  • Pixel streaming to the browser
  • Georeferenced to a stated CRS
  • Desktop, web or headset build

Interactive mapping

Every layer, on the real ground.

Web maps and map-driven explorers built on the project's own GIS: parcels, ownership, constraints, transport, amenities, catchments and footfall, queryable and filterable, drawn where they actually sit. The same layers the twin reads, published as a map anyone can open from a link, in a stated CRS with the sources named.

  • Web GIS and layer control
  • Catchments and distances
  • Points of interest and storylines
  • GeoJSON, GPKG, 3D Tiles
  • Live feeds on the map

Who it's for

Architecture, real estate and planning.

Architecture

Test the design, then show it moving.

Massing options, solar, shadows and views computed on the real site, then a walkthrough that holds up in a crit, a client meeting or a competition submission. Your Revit, Rhino and Grasshopper models go in; the twin comes back.

Real estate

Sell from the model, not the brochure.

Unit and plot browsers, 360° tours and sales-suite screens all drawn from the same scheme, so a design change updates every one of them. Buyers explore the view from their own floor before anything is built.

Planning

Evidence, not artwork.

Verified views, scenario comparison with the numbers attached, and a consultation residents can walk through from their own street. Comments are pinned in place, logged and exported straight into the planning file.

Live city twins

The city as it runs, not just as it's drawn.

We connect the feeds a place already produces, traffic counters and signal systems, bus and rail real-time data, CCTV analytics, air-quality monitors, footfall sensors and building systems, and draw them onto the real place, updating as it happens, with the history behind it so patterns become decisions.

Software integration

Works with the tools you already use.

A twin is only useful if it takes what you already have. We read Revit, Rhino and Grasshopper models, SketchUp, AutoCAD and 3ds Max files, QGIS layers, Blender scenes and Excel schedules into one georeferenced base, and hand results back in the same formats.

  • In RVT, IFC, 3DM, GH, SKP, DWG, DXF, MAX, FBX, GPKG, GeoJSON, SHP, XLSX
  • Out GLB, FBX, 3D Tiles, IFC, GeoJSON, XLSX schedules, PNG and MP4 from the twin
  • Live Grasshopper parameters and Excel mixes driving the model as sliders
