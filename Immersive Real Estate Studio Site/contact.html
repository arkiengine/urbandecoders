FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

How early in a project should we bring you in?
The earlier the better. Once you have a masterplan or concept design, we can begin — and the sooner we capture and twin, the more value you get across sales, approvals and coordination.
Do people need a VR headset to explore a scheme?
No. Our web explorers and walkthroughs run in any modern browser on phone, tablet or desktop. VR headsets and media tables are optional layers we add for exhibitions, events and experience spaces.
What does a digital twin actually include?
A geometry-accurate, data-connected 3D replica of your building or district — with context, phasing and the layers you need (zoning, transit, daylight). We keep it synced to the plan for the duration of our engagement.
Which regions do you work in?
We're based in Stockport, Greater Manchester, and work across the UK — with projects delivered internationally when the work calls for it.
How long does a typical project take?
A single web explorer or render suite can ship in 3–5 weeks. Full twin programmes and installations run 8–16 weeks depending on scale. We'll give you a firm timeline in the proposal.
