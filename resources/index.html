House rules

The same discipline in all 41.

Because the same failure keeps costing money: a confident wrong figure is indistinguishable from a right one once it is in a file. A clean-looking output is usually the failure mode.

No standards from memory

Room sizes, clearances, policy figures — the document and clause get named and marked [to verify]. The skill never supplies the number.

Provenance is visible

Stated, inferred and assumed are tagged differently and never merged. An assumption that reads as a fact is the expensive kind.

Conflicts are logged, not resolved

Two contradictory figures both get quoted with their locations. Choosing between them is yours.

No silent conversions

m² and ft², GIA and NIA, are flagged, never reconciled.

The skill informs; the architect decides

No massing, no test-fits, no recommendations dressed as analysis, no compliance verdicts.

Client material stays confidential

No web-searching the client or site unless asked; no client names in output filenames.

Gaps stay visible

A thin brief should produce a thin register and a long query sheet. That is the correct answer, not a failure.
