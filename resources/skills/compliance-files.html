Compliance & files

Checks, not verdicts.

Checking, not certifying. These produce the measuring list and the forensic read on a file — the clause gets named, the figure stays yours to confirm.

2 skills in this part of the work

Read first

These skills assist qualified practitioners. They replace neither the architect of record nor the approving authority. Every figure, clause and citation they surface is marked for you to verify — treat an unverified output as a question, not an answer.
