The platform

What a twin consists of.

Every twin starts from the same base. Options are independent and combine in any order, so the scope grows with the decision it has to support.

Full platform
Base · always included

Context model and web explorer

GIS layers, terrain, footprints and constraints in a stated CRS. A web explorer with layers, storylines and points of interest anyone can open from a link.

Option

Area context map

Where the site sits: transport, centres, landmarks with distances. Day to night intro.

Option

Phasing slider

A year slider from consent to completion, with capacity and population stepping as it moves.

Option

Use-stack X-ray

Cut a block to reveal the use mix by floor. Click a floor for its area schedule.

Option

Scenario compare

Two or three options side by side: density, daylight, views, transit access, numbers recomputing.

Option

Day, night and seasons

Lighting and weather states for shadow studies, night-time economy and atmosphere.

Option

Live city feeds

Traffic counters, CCTV counts, GTFS-RT, DEFRA AURN, footfall and rail on the model.

Option

Engagement layer

Residents comment in place. Moderation, export to the planning file, committee summary.

Option

Data integration

Planning portal, GIS server, asset register or IoT platform feeding the twin so it stays current.

Option

Media table and VR

Touch table, kiosk or headset build of the same twin for exhibitions and consultation rooms.

How we work

From site boundary to running twin in four moves.

01
Discover

Read the site and the decision

We audit what exists, GIS, BIM, surveys, open data and live feeds, and agree what the twin has to answer and for whom.

02
Assemble

Build the context

Terrain, parcels, networks and constraints cleaned into one georeferenced base in a stated CRS. Every later layer sits on it.

03
Model

Massing, scenarios, feeds

Options generated on the base, feeds connected, engagement wired in. Reviewed with you at each milestone.

04
Run

Deploy and keep current

Web, media table or headset. We hand over the model and keep it synced to the plan and the feeds after launch.
