Stockport East Masterplan

What it does. A working twin of the Stockport East regeneration area. GIS layers, key views, dynamic sun and weather, first-person and drone walkthroughs, measuring and section tools, solar and viewshed analysis, POI and transport data, all on one georeferenced base.

What it returns. A model that officers, designers and residents can move through and question. Every capability on this site was recorded inside it. Hover to play the flythrough.

Park and Urban Space Configurator

What it does. Plot area, unit mix, green-space ratio and block typology on live sliders. The park and the blocks around it regenerate directly in the twin, on the real site.

What it returns. Residential units, commercial tenants and green-space ratio recomputed as you drag: 1,200 m² plot, 25 homes, 10 commercial tenants and 35% green space in the configuration shown.

City twinTokyo · PLATEAU

Tokyo Digital Twin

What it does. Central Tokyo assembled from PLATEAU city data and photogrammetric context: Chiyoda, Chuo and Minato wards with their boundaries, land use and transport, every building carrying its own record, and sun, weather, viewshed and sun-hours analysis running on top.

What it returns. Click a block and the record opens — Shiodome City Center, code 13103-bldg-21730, 225.8 m. Hover to play the day running across the city.

Chiyoda · Chuo · Minato·PLATEAU building records·Unreal Engine 5·boundaries, POIs, transport
ConfiguratorTokyo landmarks

Metabolist Landmark Configurators

What it does. Three Metabolist landmarks rebuilt as live definitions on their real sites: the Nakagin Capsule Tower, the Shizuoka Press and Broadcasting Tower and the Takara Beautilion. Floors, cores, wings, capsule fill and orientation on sliders — or asked for in plain language.

What it returns. The capsule tower reports 177 capsules over 1,421.1 m², towers at 61 m and 49.8 m with three bridges. Ask it for twenty storeys and it re-solves in about twenty seconds.

How we work

From site boundary to running twin in four moves.

Every twin, from a single site study to a city programme, runs through the same four moves.

01
Discover

Read the site and the decision

We audit what exists, GIS, BIM, surveys, open data and live feeds, and agree what the twin has to answer and for whom.

02
Assemble

Build the context

Terrain, parcels, networks and constraints cleaned into one georeferenced base in a stated CRS. Every later layer sits on it.

03
Model

Massing, scenarios, feeds

Options generated on the base, feeds connected, engagement wired in. Reviewed with you at each milestone.

04
Run

Deploy and keep current

Web, media table or headset. We hand over the model and keep it synced to the plan and the feeds after launch.
