Capabilities

City engagement and planning, built on a living twin.

All services

Digital Twins

Living, data-synced replicas of buildings, campuses and city districts — BIM, GIS and live feeds bound into one navigable model that stays current as the plan moves.

Immersive Experiences

VR journeys, AR overlays and real-time walkthroughs built on game engines — headset, touch table or a link that opens in a browser on any device.

Planning & Masterplanning

Scenario testing in 3D — massing, density, phasing, daylight, views and transit. Compare options side by side and take the evidence into the committee room.

Community Engagement

Public-facing consultation that lets residents explore the proposal, leave comments in place and be heard — turning objection into informed participation.

City Explorer

A web-based explorer for your place — layers, storylines and points of interest anyone can navigate from a link. No install, no training, no gatekeeping.

GIS, City Data & Context

Site and city context assembled from GIS layers, open data and survey records — georeferenced to a stated CRS and cleaned into the base every twin, application and visualisation is built on.

Media Tables & Installations

Interactive touch tables, kiosks and projected installations that turn a model into something a room full of people can gather around and actually use.

Place Management Platform

A live dashboard on top of the twin — assets, occupancy, environmental and footfall data — so the people running a place can see how it actually performs.

Gamified Learning

Game-engine tools that teach a place — school programmes, public exhibitions and training that make planning and the built environment something people want to explore.

02 / Flagship Product

Arki Engine

Arki Engine is the flagship product of Urban Decoders' research and development. An Unreal Engine–powered, real-time design platform that brings architectural and spatial ideas to life through advanced data-driven workflows, configurable design systems, and complex geometric and analytical computation.

All wrapped in a high-fidelity visualisation environment that streamlines material choices, configuration options, and overall design efficiency — from parametric pavilions to city-scale masterplans.

How we work

From consultation to launch in four moves.

01
Discovery
